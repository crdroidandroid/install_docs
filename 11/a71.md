# Installation Guide for crDroid on Samsung Galaxy A71 (SM-A715F)

---

## Requirements

- Unlocked bootloader
- Custom recovery 
- A computer with ADB & Fastboot installed
- Backup your data – clean flash will erase everything

---

## Files to Download

- crDroid ROM (Latest version)
- GApps package (optional): MindTheGApps 

---

## Installation Steps

### 1.Boot into recovery mode

- Power off the device  
- Press **Volume Up + Power** to enter recovery

---

### 2.Format data (recommended for clean flash)

- In recovery, go to Wipe > Format Data
- Type yes and confirm

---

### 3. Flash crDroid ROM

- Tap Install
- Select the crDroid .zip and swipe to flash
- (Optional) Flash GApps right after

---

### 4. Reboot to system

- Tap Reboot > System
- Wait for first boot (can take a few minutes)
